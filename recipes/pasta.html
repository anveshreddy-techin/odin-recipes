Pasta

Description

Pasta is a classic Italian dish made from wheat dough and served with a variety of sauces and seasonings.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Boil water and cook the pasta.
  2. Prepare the sauce in a separate pan.
  3. Drain the cooked pasta.
  4. Mix pasta with the sauce.
  5. Top with cheese and serve.
