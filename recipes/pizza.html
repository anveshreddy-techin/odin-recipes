Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a baked dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Vegetable or meat toppings

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly.
  4. Add cheese and toppings.
  5. Bake until golden brown.
  6. Slice and serve hot.
